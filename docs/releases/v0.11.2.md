# Phase 11 improvements — Who said what, and videos without captions (v0.11.2)

**BYTE v0.11.2** tells speakers apart in recordings, and can summarize YouTube videos that have no captions. Both
happen on your Mac. It has everything from v0.11.1 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Speaker labels**: attach a recording of a meeting, interview or call, and the transcript reads "Speaker 1
  [0:00]: …", "Speaker 2 [0:42]: …". Then ask "what did Speaker 2 agree to?" or "summarize each person's view".
  - It needs two small models (32 MB), downloaded once from Settings → Models → Voice → "Label who's speaking".
  - The labels are BYTE's best guess. Two similar voices can be merged, and one voice can sometimes be split in two.
  - It's on by default once the models are there. Your own dictation through 🎤 is never labelled.
- **Videos without captions**: when a YouTube video has no captions, BYTE gets its audio with the video helper and
  transcribes it on your Mac. Then it summarizes the video with chapters and timestamps, like any other video. The
  video card says the transcript was made from the audio.
  - **Video helper** (Settings → Models → Voice): yt-dlp, free and open source, about 36 MB, from its official
    GitHub release. BYTE checks the download against the release's checksums before using it.
  - YouTube changes often, and **Update** gets the newest yt-dlp, which fixes most errors. Videos up to 3 hours.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts, voice input, and
  speaker labels (new).

- Slides made from a video (key frames): later.
