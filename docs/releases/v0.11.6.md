# Phase 11 improvements — Notes, web clipper and mind maps (v0.11.6)

**BYTE v0.11.6** adds notes you own, a one-click web clipper, and mind maps of any answer. It has everything from
v0.11.5 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **📝 Notes** (the notebook button in the top bar, or ⌘K → Notes):
  - Markdown notes with folders, tags and search; edit and preview.
  - They're plain files you own in **Documents/BYTE/Notes**: they open in any editor or Obsidian. You can pick
    another folder in Settings.
  - **📝 under any answer** saves it as a note (title and tags from the chat, citation marks removed).
  - **Ask BYTE** about a note; **Show in Finder**; deleting moves a note to the Trash.
  - With the knowledge base on, your notes folder is part of it, so BYTE can answer from your notes.
- **Web clipper:** drag **Clip to BYTE** (Settings → About → Notes and web clipper) to your browser's bookmarks bar.
  - On any page, click it: BYTE reads the page itself and saves it as a note in **Clips**, with the source link and
    any text you selected quoted at the top. A notification says when it's done.
  - Works in Safari, Chrome and Firefox; Safari asks "Open BYTE?" the first time.
- **🧠 Mind maps** (the branch button under any longer answer, or in a note):
  - Headings and lists become branches right away, without a model. Plain prose is outlined by the model on your
    Mac (even the smallest models do it well).
  - Click a branch to fold it; zoom in and out.
  - Export as PNG or SVG, copy as an outline, or save it as a note.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts, voice input,
  speaker labels, spoken answers, hands-free talking, “Hey BYTE”, a catalog of natural voices, and now notes, a web
  clipper and mind maps.

## Not in this version yet
- The brainstorm board, the help center, example prompts and personality settings: v0.11.7 (finishes Phase 11).
- Notes don't sync between Macs by themselves; keeping the notes folder in iCloud Drive (Change folder…) does that.
