# Phase 10 improvements — Tasks, schedules and a daily briefing (v0.10.1)

**BYTE v0.10.1** adds a to-do list with reminders, a daily briefing, and questions BYTE asks on a schedule. It has
everything from v0.10.0 (Mac upkeep) too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **✅ Tasks** (a new button in the top bar): add to-dos with a due date, a reminder and a repeat (every day,
  weekdays, week or month); check them off; overdue ones come first.
  - In chat: "add pay rent by Friday to my to-do list", "what's on my to-do list?", "mark pay rent as done".
  - Reminders are Mac notifications. "Remind me to…" still uses Apple Reminders when Mac control is on.
- **Daily briefing**: "brief me" gives today's calendar, reminders and to-dos, the weather where you live, and
  headlines on topics you follow, each with its source.
  - BYTE puts it together itself from what it read, so nothing in it is made up, and it works even with no model
    loaded.
  - Pick up to 5 news topics in Settings → Features → Daily briefing topics.
- **Schedules**: "every weekday at 7:30, brief me" or "every weekday at 8am, summarize the latest AI news".
  - BYTE asks you first, then does it on its own while BYTE is open. A run missed while BYTE was closed happens
    once when you next open it.
  - Each answer is saved as a chat, and you get a notification.
  - Manage them in the ✅ panel: Run now, switch on or off, open the last answer.
- **Translation** is more faithful with small models (the same words every time, not creative ones).

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**: Apple apps, Mail, Messages, selected text, clipboard, files, Terminal, storage
  clean-up, health checks, uninstalling, login items.
- **Tasks and schedules (new)**: to-dos with reminders, a daily briefing, scheduled questions.

## Not in this version yet
News digests and page watchers with price alerts (v0.10.2), multi-step automations and making Shortcuts (v0.10.3),
trackers, connectors and dashboards (later v0.10.x). BYTE only runs schedules while it's open; starting at login
comes with the automations.
