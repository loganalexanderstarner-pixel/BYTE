# Windows test build 9: BYTE starts again, and the help is organised (v0.12.6-win.9)

**A test build of BYTE for Windows 10 and 11**, built from the Windows branch. It is not the finished Windows app: expect rough edges and tell us what you find. The Mac app is the same as v0.12.6.

## Installing
1. Download `BYTE_0.12.6-win.9_x64-setup.exe` below (about 560 MB; it carries every engine).
2. Run it. Windows will say it "protected your PC" because the build is not signed with a paid certificate: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for you only, with no administrator rights. If you already have a test build, it updates in place.
3. Open BYTE.

## Fixed
- **BYTE no longer closes by itself when it starts.** Test builds 6, 7 and 8 stopped right at launch on a real PC (the PC-control part was started before the app was ready). This was found by running the installed app.
- **"How do I turn on Hey BYTE?"** now finds its help article.

## New
- **Help is organised by what you need:** Start here, Chat and research, Talking to BYTE, Controlling your PC, Models and speed, Account and privacy, Fixing problems. Searching still shows one list.
- **A new README** that covers every BYTE app (Mac, Windows, Android and BYTE Cloud).

Everything from test builds 6 to 8 is included (a faster Hey BYTE, PC control, the browser, screen questions, guided help and the Tutorial). Everything that listens, looks at your screen or controls your PC is **off until you turn it on**.
