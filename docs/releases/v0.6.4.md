# Phase 6 improvements — Kitchen and Web Auto (v0.6.4)

**BYTE v0.6.4** is a Phase 6 improvement build. It has everything from v0.6.3 plus the Kitchen and a Web Auto
setting. See all versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Web: Off / Auto / Always.** Click the Web button in the chat box to switch. Auto (default) searches when a
  question needs it; Always searches for every real question; Off stays offline.
- **Kitchen: a professional chef for home cooks.**
  - "What can I make with eggs, spinach and feta?": dishes to choose from, each saying what else it needs.
  - "How do I make a flat white?": a recipe card with a photo (from real recipe pages when Web is on), servings
    you can change, ingredients to tick off, steps with timers and "how to tell it's done", tips and swaps.
    Coffee and baking recipes get exact grams, ratios and temperatures.
  - "Plan dinners for the week with chicken and rice": a week of meals and a grocery list that skips what you
    have.
  - **Recipe box** (📖): save recipes you like, search them, save as PDF. Ask for "my saved …" any time.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources** (Off/Auto/Always), **deep research with papers and citation styles**,
  **fact-checks and comparisons**, **places nearby and trip plans**, **recipes and meal plans** (Phase 6).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).
