# Phase 6 improvements — Fact-check and compare (v0.6.1)

**BYTE v0.6.1** is a Phase 6 improvement build. It has everything from v0.6.0 plus fact-checking and
comparisons. See all versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Fact-check.** Ask "Is it true that…?", "Fact-check this: …", or press the shield button under any answer.
  BYTE checks each claim against sources for and against it, and answers with a table: a verdict badge
  (True, Mostly true, Mixed, Mostly false, False, Unproven) and the exact quote that settles each claim.
- **Compare & decide.** Ask "MacBook Air vs Dell XPS for college?", "Should I get X or Y?" or "Compare X, Y and Z".
  BYTE researches each option and shows a score table with a slider for how much each thing matters to you;
  the totals re-rank as you move them. Then it recommends one and says what would change the pick.

Works in any mode with Web on; Deep and Extended read more pages.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources**, **deep research with papers and citation styles** (v0.6.0), **fact-checks and
  comparisons** (v0.6.1).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).
