# Windows test build 7: a tutorial, guided help and PC-control limits you choose (v0.12.6-win.7)

**A test build of BYTE for Windows 10 and 11**, built from the Windows branch. It is not the finished Windows app: expect rough edges and tell us what you find. The Mac app is the same as v0.12.6.

## Installing
1. Download `BYTE_0.12.6-win.7_x64-setup.exe` below (about 560 MB; it carries every engine). On a Snapdragon (ARM) PC, take the `arm64` one.
2. Run it. Windows will say it "protected your PC" because the build is not signed with a paid certificate: choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It installs for you only, with no administrator rights. If you already have a test build, it updates in place.
3. Open BYTE. It looks at your PC, recommends a model that fits it and downloads it.

## New since test build 6
- **Tutorial** (Help → Tutorial): one short tutorial per feature (17 of them) and a "Do the whole tutorial" button, with progress remembered, example phrases, buttons that open the right setting, and live checks. Nothing is switched on for you.
- **Help updated for Windows:** every article reviewed for PC wording, plus new articles on hands-free use, interrupting BYTE and calls/games, PC control, the browser, the screen features, where the model lives, commands or conversation, and guided help.
- **Guided help** (Settings → Control and screen, off by default): ask how to do something in the window in front of you, then choose **Show me as I go** (a ring shows each next button; say "next" or "skip", F9 or "look again" re-reads the window) or **Do it for me**. It never rings or clicks password or card fields.
- **PC control limits you choose:** how long one run may take (90 seconds up to **Unlimited**), steps in one plan (12 up to **Unlimited**), and tries to re-plan (2 up to **Unlimited**).
- **Never ask, just do it:** an off-by-default switch that turns off every approval card for PC control. Program files (.exe, scripts, installers) and a few dangerous key combinations stay refused, and Stop always works.
- **A cheaper "Hey BYTE" check:** BYTE no longer runs speech recognition on sounds that cannot be the phrase (keyboard, fans, music), and the listening pill says "In a call: Hey BYTE is off" during a call.
- This is a Windows-only test build made with lighter optimization so it builds faster; there is no Mac or ARM64 installer in this release.

## Everything new has its own switch
Open **Settings**. Settings now has groups on the left (General, Models and speed, Voice and Hey BYTE, Control and screen, Privacy, Memory and notes, Account and cloud, Backup and help) and a search box. Anything that listens, looks at your screen or controls your PC is **off until you turn it on**.

## Hey BYTE
- **Nothing pops up.** BYTE answers out loud, like a speaker. (Settings → Voice: "Show a window when I say Hey BYTE" if you want the small window.)
- **Say it in one breath** ("Hey BYTE, what's the weather") and it answers at once. It keeps listening for your next sentence on the same microphone, so there is no second microphone to open, and the check that you said the phrase is faster.
- **Waits until you're done.** If you stop on "and", "but", "um", it gives you one more second and joins what you say next.
- **A listening pill and a chime you choose** (system, soft, none).
- **Talk key** (Settings → Voice): press a key to talk to BYTE without the phrase. Suggested: Ctrl+Alt+Space.
- **Barge-in** (off by default): interrupt BYTE by speaking. "Headphones" stops BYTE when you speak; "Speakers" subtracts BYTE's own voice from the microphone first and needs tuning in your room.
- **Focus on one voice** (off by default): while you talk to BYTE it ignores other voices (a phone call, the TV). Say "listen to everyone", "also listen to <name>" (a taught voice) or "stop focusing on me". The voice check is a simple one and is not tuned yet.
- **Quiet during calls and games** (Settings → Voice and Hey BYTE): Hey BYTE stays off while another program uses the microphone for a call, and has no chime while a game runs.
- The cloud voice is steadier (a length limit stops drawn-out endings).

## Control your PC (Settings → Control and screen)
- **How BYTE handles commands:** *Smart* acts on clear commands ("set the volume to 30", "open Spotify") and just talks about everything else ("how do I turn off the wifi?"). *Always* is the old behavior. *Chat only* acts only when you start with "do:". It works the same typed or spoken.
- **PC control** (off): BYTE reads the front window as a list of buttons and boxes and clicks, types and presses keys for you. It asks before anything risky (shutdown, closing windows, opening files, links other than websites). A banner shows while it is working, with a Stop button. Pick the model it uses (your chat model, or a model you choose from a list of capable ones), and keep it in system memory while a game runs.
- **Routines** (off): "game time" runs several steps; add and remove them in a menu, or schedule them.
- **Find files by voice** (off): searches only folders you choose, then opens File Explorer with the file selected.
- **How's my PC?**: a short spoken status.
- **Browser** (off): research with the browser shown while BYTE works, or let BYTE drive a separate Edge or Chrome window. It asks before sending or buying, never types passwords, blocks banking and similar sites, and keeps a log of the sites it visited.

## Look at my screen (off)
- **Ask about the screen:** "what do you think of this page?", "explain this error".
- **Replay:** keeps the last 30 seconds of your screen **in memory only** (never saved), so you can ask "where did I go wrong?" after a fight or a round. A badge shows while it is recording. Apps like password managers and banking pages are never captured.
- **Game advisor** and **read this to me.**

## Everyday helpers
Disk hints before a download, with a "Free up space" card (it asks before deleting anything); a separate learned style per person you taught; spoken notices; "where did I leave off", voice notes and "good morning"; a daily spoken briefing; spoken translate; a portable **backup and restore** of your settings and chats; and a **problem report** button that scrubs your name, paths and keys and shows you exactly what it includes before saving.

## Where the model lives (Settings → Models and speed)
Automatic, graphics memory only, graphics + system memory, or processor only (keeps your graphics card free for games).

## Known to be rough
- Not tried yet with a real microphone and speakers on every PC. Barge-in, voice focus and the pill have only been tested with synthetic audio.
- PC control, the browser and the screen features have not been run against real apps and games yet. Capture works for windowed and borderless games; exclusive full-screen may not.
- Texts and reading Outlook mail are not on Windows yet. Spotify playback needs Spotify signed in.
- Graphics: NVIDIA (CUDA) and AMD/Intel (Vulkan) are tested on one NVIDIA card.
