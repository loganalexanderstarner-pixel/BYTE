# Phase 11 improvements — A voice catalog and more human speech (v0.11.5)

**BYTE v0.11.5** lets you pick BYTE's voice from a catalog of over 2,000 free voices from five open projects,
speaks more like a person, and makes “Hey BYTE” a real hands-free assistant. It has everything from v0.11.4 too.
See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Browse voices** (Settings → Models → Voice → BYTE's voice): 167 free downloads with over 2,000 voices in 77
  languages, from five open projects. No account, nothing sent anywhere.
  - **Kokoro:** the most natural; 28 American and British voices (also in a smaller version).
  - **Piper:** hundreds of voices in about 40 languages, small and quick, including packs with 100+ accents.
  - **Kitten TTS:** tiny (about 30 MB) with eight expressive voices; good next to a big model.
  - **Supertonic 3:** very fast, ten speaking styles, 31 languages.
  - **Pocket TTS (Kyutai):** speaks in the tone of a real recording, so it sounds more like a person.
  - Filter by language, female or male, provider, Expressive, Light on memory and Downloaded. Every card says
    what it sounds like, its download size, how much memory it uses while speaking, and its license.
  - Hear a sample before downloading; after downloading, ▶ any voice and click its name to use it.
- **More human speech:**
  - When an answer will be heard, BYTE answers like a person talking: the answer first, short sentences, a natural
    reaction where it fits, and no tables or links read out.
  - Steps are said as “First… Second… Third…”.
  - Natural pauses between sentences and longer ones between paragraphs; questions a touch slower; no clicks
    between pieces.
  - **How BYTE speaks:** Calm, Natural or Lively.
- **Light on your Mac:** voices run on the CPU, never on the GPU your chat model uses, only while BYTE talks, with
  at most half the cores. Memory while speaking is about 140 MB (Kitten nano) to 600 MB (Pocket). When the Mac is
  short on memory, BYTE suggests a lighter voice.
- **Voice on this Mac, or in the cloud:** with BYTE Cloud connected, choose where the voice is made. Cloud voices
  use no memory on the Mac (they need the cluster to offer them; until then, and in private chats, the Mac's
  voice is used).
- **“Hey BYTE” answers out loud:** say “Hey BYTE, …” and BYTE answers aloud even with Read answers aloud off,
  then listens a few seconds for a follow-up (“and tomorrow?”). Say “thanks” to end. With **Open at login** on,
  it's ready as soon as you log in; the setting has a one-click link for it.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts, voice input,
  speaker labels, spoken answers, hands-free talking, “Hey BYTE”, and a catalog of natural voices.

## Not in this version yet
- Notes, the web clipper and mind maps: v0.11.6.
- The brainstorm board, the help center, example prompts and personality settings: v0.11.7.
- Voices with strong emotion (laughs, excitement) are too big to run next to a chat model on a 16 GB Mac; they
  will come through BYTE Cloud once the cluster offers voices.
- Speaking needs a Mac for now.
