# Phase 9 improvements — Mail and Messages (v0.9.1)

**BYTE v0.9.1** adds Mail and Messages to Mac control: BYTE checks your email, writes replies and new emails, and
gets texts ready. It never sends anything itself: you read it and press Send. It has everything from v0.9.0 too.
See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Check your email**: "any new emails?", "summarize my inbox", "did Sam email me?", "emails from Jen this week".
  BYTE reads them in Mail and tells you which ones need a reply first.
- **Reply to an email**: "Reply to Sam's email saying I can make it and I'll bring dessert". BYTE finds Sam's latest
  email, writes the reply, shows it to you, and opens it in Mail ready to send.
- **Write a new email**: "email Jen about the party on Saturday". BYTE finds Jen's address in Contacts; if there are
  two Jens, it asks which one.
- **Texts**: "text Mom that I'm running late". Messages opens to Mom with your words filled in (they're on the
  clipboard too). You press Send.
- As always, you see exactly what BYTE will do and say **Do it** first. A draft email can be removed with **Undo**.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research (Phase 6)**, **writing and learning (Phase 7)**, **speed and tuning (Phase 8)**, **memory**, **your
  files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control (Phase 9)**: Reminders, Calendar, Notes, Music, Shortcuts, Safari, system settings, Mail and
  Messages.

## Not in this version yet
Still to come in Phase 9: a hotkey for selected text anywhere, clipboard history, finding and organizing files, a
Finder "Ask BYTE" action and a terminal helper. BYTE reads the start of each email (not attachments). Then
automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and polish (v0.12).
