# Phase 10 improvements — Connectors: Obsidian, Notion and calendars (v0.10.5)

**BYTE v0.10.5** can use your notes and calendars: your Obsidian vault, your Notion pages, and calendars you
subscribe to by link, such as Google Calendar. None of them need an account with BYTE. Secrets stay in your Mac's
Keychain, and BYTE only reads or adds things when you ask. It has everything from v0.10.4 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Settings → Connectors**: a place to set up each connector. Each one stays off until you do.
- **Obsidian**: pick your vault folder, then ask "what do my Obsidian notes say about sourdough?". BYTE answers from
  your notes and cites them; each source opens the note in Obsidian.
  - "Add a note to Obsidian: Groceries — milk, eggs", or "save that to Obsidian" to keep BYTE's last answer.
  - New notes go in a BYTE folder inside your vault, after your OK, and Undo removes them. Notes you already have
    are never changed.
- **Notion**: make a free integration at notion.so/my-integrations, paste its secret, and share the pages BYTE may
  use with it.
  - "Search Notion for the Lisbon trip" reads the matching pages and answers with links to them.
  - "Add a page to Notion: Packing list — passport, charger", or "save that to Notion", after your OK.
  - The secret goes straight into your Mac's Keychain; it's never saved in BYTE's settings.
- **Calendar links**: paste a calendar's private iCal address, such as Google Calendar's "Secret address in iCal
  format".
  - Its events appear in your daily briefing.
  - When Mac control is off, "what's on my calendar tomorrow?" and "my schedule this week" read them too.
  - Repeating events, all-day events and cancelled ones are handled.
  - Read-only: BYTE never changes these calendars, and the addresses are kept in the Keychain.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Tasks, schedules, feeds, watchers, automations and trackers**.
- **Connectors (new)**: Obsidian, Notion, calendar links.

## Not in this version yet
Google (Gmail, Drive, Calendar), Dropbox, OneDrive and Spotify: these need BYTE registered with each company first,
so they come later. Dashboards (v0.10.6) finish Phase 10. Notion and calendar links need a Mac, because their secrets
are kept in the macOS Keychain.
