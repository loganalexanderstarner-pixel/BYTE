# Phase 11 improvements — BYTE talks back, and “Hey BYTE” (v0.11.3)

**BYTE v0.11.3** can read its answers aloud, hold a hands-free conversation, and wake up when you say “Hey BYTE”.
Everything happens on your Mac, with your Mac's own voices. It has everything from v0.11.2 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **🔊 on every answer** reads it aloud; click again to stop.
  - BYTE reads the words and skips code, tables and links: it says "there's code on screen" instead.
  - Long answers are read up to about a minute, and the rest stays on screen.
- **Read answers aloud** (Settings → Models → Voice): BYTE speaks every answer. Pick the voice and speed, and use
  **Try it** to hear it. More voices come from macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Manage
  Voices.
- **Talk with BYTE** (the sound-wave button in the message box) is a hands-free conversation:
  1. Say something. BYTE notices when you pause.
  2. BYTE sends your question, then answers out loud.
  3. BYTE listens again.

  Say "stop" or "that's all", or press Esc, to end it; it also ends after 20 seconds of quiet. It works in Quick Ask
  too.
- **“Hey BYTE”** (Settings → Models → Voice, off until you turn it on): say “Hey BYTE” from anywhere and Quick Ask
  opens with a chime, already listening. Your next sentence is sent.
  - While it's on, the microphone stays on and macOS shows its orange dot.
  - Only short bursts of speech are checked, on your Mac, and nothing is kept.
  - It pauses while BYTE is talking or you're recording.
  - It needs a speech model (the Quick English one is enough).

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts, voice input,
  speaker labels, and now spoken answers, hands-free talking and “Hey BYTE”.

## Not in this version yet

- The brainstorm board, the help center, example prompts and personality settings: v0.11.5.
- Reading aloud and “Hey BYTE” need a Mac for now.
