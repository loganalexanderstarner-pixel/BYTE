# Phase 12 improvements — Kids mode, backups and erasing (v0.12.1)

**BYTE v0.12.1** ships inside v0.12.3 (no separate download). It adds a kids mode, encrypted backups to iCloud Drive, automatic deletion of old chats, and an
"erase everything" button. It has everything from v0.12.0 too. See all versions in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Kids mode** (Settings → Privacy):
  - set a 4–6 digit PIN and turn it on;
  - BYTE answers in a way that's right for children, with bigger text and fun ideas on the home screen;
  - no web, Mac control, files, terminal, connectors or automations, and no grown-up memories in answers;
  - kids only see their own chats (kept in a "Kids" folder);
  - settings can't be changed, and it's enforced in BYTE's core, not just hidden;
  - **Grown-ups** at the top turns it off with the PIN, and too many wrong PINs mean a wait.
- **Backups** (Settings → Privacy):
  - **Back up now** makes one encrypted file with your chats, memories, settings and notes;
  - with iCloud Drive on, it's saved in iCloud Drive → BYTE Backups, so it's safe if your Mac is lost;
  - only your passphrase opens it;
  - **Back up every week**, with the passphrase kept in your Keychain; the newest 5 are kept;
  - **Restore…** brings a backup back and restarts BYTE. Your current chats are set aside, not deleted.
- **Old chats:** delete chats you haven't used in 30, 90, 180 or 365 days. Pinned chats are always kept.
- **Erase everything:** type ERASE (and Touch ID when the lock is on). BYTE starts over as new; models and your
  notes files stay.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, voice input, spoken answers,
  “Hey BYTE”, 2,000+ natural voices, notes, a web clipper, mind maps, a brainstorm board, personality and help.
- **Privacy:** an offline switch, a Touch ID lock, an activity log, kids mode, encrypted backups and erasing.
