# Phase 11 — Quick Ask, menu bar and command palette (v0.11.0)

**BYTE v0.11.0** starts Phase 11: input and windows. You can ask BYTE from any app without switching to it, find
BYTE in your menu bar, and jump to anything in BYTE with ⌘K. It has everything from v0.10.6 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Quick Ask**: press **⌥Space** in any app and a small BYTE window opens on top. Type your question and the answer
  appears right there.
  - **Esc** hides it, and clicking anywhere else hides it too. ⌘N starts a new question.
  - **Open in BYTE** continues the chat in the main window. Quick Ask chats are saved like any other chat.
- **BYTE in the menu bar**: click the icon for Quick Ask; its menu shows BYTE or quits it.
- **Command palette (⌘K)**: type a few letters to find any chat, a settings page, a mode, a theme or a panel
  (documents, tasks, writing studio, study and more), then press Enter.
- **Your own shortcuts**: Settings → About → **Keyboard and menu bar**. Click a shortcut and press new keys to change
  Quick Ask's (⌥Space) or the selected-text hotkey (⌥⌘B). You can also turn Quick Ask or the menu-bar icon off. The
  same place lists BYTE's in-app keys.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows and keys (new):** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette and custom shortcuts.
