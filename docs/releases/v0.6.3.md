# Phase 6 improvements — Faster research (v0.6.3)

**BYTE v0.6.3** is a Phase 6 improvement build. It has everything from v0.6.2, with faster research and a
setting to read more. See all versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Faster research.** Deep and Extended start searching while they plan, only pause between searches where a
  search engine requires it, and reuse searches, papers and places found in the last hour (pages for a day), so
  follow-up questions and Regenerate are quicker.
- **Research depth.** Settings → Engine → Research: Normal, More or Max. More and Max read up to 32 pages in
  Deep and 48 in Extended for more thorough answers (they take longer).

Nothing to set up: no accounts, keys or servers.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources**, **deep research with papers and citation styles**, **fact-checks and
  comparisons**, **places nearby and trip plans** (Phase 6).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
YouTube summaries (v0.6.4), a web agent (v0.6.5); then writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8),
controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final
polish (v0.12).
