# Phase 10 improvements — News feeds and page watchers (v0.10.2)

**BYTE v0.10.2** follows your favorite sites and watches pages for you: it catches you up on new stories and
sends a notification when a page changes or a price drops. It has everything from v0.10.1 (tasks, schedules and
the daily briefing) too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **News feeds**: "follow theverge.com" (a site's address is enough: BYTE finds its RSS or Atom feed), then
  "what's new in my feeds?".
  - The digest lists each site's new stories with a short summary and a link to the source. BYTE puts it together
    from the feeds themselves, so nothing in it is made up, and it works with no model loaded.
  - Each digest shows only what's new since the last one.
  - For a digest every morning, add a schedule: "every day at 7am, what's new in my feeds".
  - "What feeds do I follow?", "unfollow The Verge".
- **Watched pages**: "watch example.com/careers" or "tell me when example.com/careers changes".
  - BYTE checks the page every few hours (or "every hour", "daily") and sends a notification with what's new on it.
  - Menus, dates and counters don't count as changes.
- **Price alerts**: "tell me when <link> drops below $200" or "track the price of <link>".
  - The price comes from the store page's own product data, never a guess. With a target, you get a notification
    when the price reaches it; without one, whenever it drops.
  - Some big stores block automated visits; BYTE says so in the ✅ panel when it can't read a price.
- Adding a watched page shows an approval card first, since BYTE then checks it on its own.
- **Everything is in the ✅ panel**: follow feeds, see how many new stories each has, watch pages, check now,
  pause, or stop.
- Switch it off in Settings → Features → **News feeds and watched pages**. It needs web access.
- Fixed: a scheduled daily briefing used the web even when web access was off.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**: Apple apps, Mail, Messages, selected text, clipboard, files, Terminal, storage
  clean-up, health checks, uninstalling, login items.
- **Tasks and schedules**: to-dos with reminders, a daily briefing, scheduled questions.
- **Feeds and watchers (new)**: news digests, page-change and price alerts.

## Not in this version yet
Multi-step automations and making Shortcuts (v0.10.3), then trackers, connectors and dashboards (later v0.10.x).
BYTE only checks feeds and pages while it's open; starting at login comes with the automations. Stores that show
prices only after running scripts, or that block automated visits, can't be price-watched yet.
