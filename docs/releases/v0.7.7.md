# Phase 7 — Long-form writing and your own style (v0.7.7)

**BYTE v0.7.7** completes Phase 7: BYTE now writes longer pieces from a plan you control, and can write like you.
It has everything from v0.7.6 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Write something new** (✍️ writing studio → second tab).
  - Pick an essay, story, blog post, report, speech or poem, say what it's about and how long.
  - BYTE plans it first. Rename, reorder, remove or add parts, then **Write it**: it's written part by part, and
    each part picks up where the last one ended.
  - Speeches are timed at about 130 words a minute and end with a call to action.
  - Poems come in free verse, rhyming, haiku, sonnet or limerick.
  - When it's done: **Save** (a Markdown file), **Copy**, or **Edit it** in the studio.
- **Write like me.**
  - **Teach BYTE your style**: paste one to three things you wrote. BYTE describes how you write, and you can read
    and edit that description.
  - With **Write like me** on, rewrites, expansions and new pieces sound like you. Fix grammar never changes your
    style.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research (Phase 6)**: web search, deep research, fact-checks, comparisons, places, trips, recipes, YouTube, a
  web agent, reviews, prices, game hints.
- **Writing and learning (Phase 7, complete)**: flashcards, quizzes, tutor mode, the writing studio, long pieces in
  your style, translation, job search, custom assistants.
- **Memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents** (PDF, PowerPoint, Word).

## Not in this version yet
Phase 8 (speed and a tuning panel, v0.8), controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask
windows (v0.11), Touch ID and polish (v0.12). Long pieces are best with a model of 3B or more; very small models
repeat themselves.
