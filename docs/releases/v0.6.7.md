# Phase 6 — Reviews, prices, game hints and self-check (v0.6.7)

**BYTE v0.6.7** finishes Phase 6 (research). It has everything from v0.6.6 too. See all versions and what each
one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Reviews**: "Reviews of the Sony WH-1000XM6", "is the Kindle Colorsoft worth it?". You get a card with the
  verdict, star ratings from the sites, pros and cons with their sources, who it suits and who should skip it.
- **Price compare**: "Cheapest place to buy AirPods Pro 3?". Prices come straight from the stores' own pages
  (never guessed), cheapest first, with stock and refurbished/used marked, and when they were checked.
- **Game hints**: "I'm stuck on the Water Temple in Ocarina of Time". Hints get stronger one tap at a time, and
  the full solution sits behind a spoiler button.
- **Answers checked against their sources**: after Deep, Extended and fact-check answers, BYTE flags any cited
  claim its source doesn't clearly back.
- **Best of 3 for hard questions**: maths, logic and puzzles in Deep or Extended get three drafts, and you see the
  answer most of them agree on.

All need Web on (Auto or Always), except best of 3. Each can be switched off in Settings → About → Features.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Research (Phase 6, complete)**: web search with sources, deep research with papers and citation styles,
  fact-checks, comparisons, places nearby, trip plans, recipes and meal plans, YouTube summaries, a web agent,
  reviews, prices and game hints.
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
Writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8), controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations and price alerts
(v0.10), voice, video-to-slides and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final polish (v0.12).
