# Phase 5 — Documents on this Mac (v0.5.0)

**BYTE v0.5.0** is the Phase 5 build. It has everything from Phases 1–5. See all versions and what each one
has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Documents made on your Mac.** The 📄 button (top right) now works without the cloud. Choose PDF report,
  Slides or Word document, say what it's about, optionally add a source file or let BYTE research the web and
  cite sources. BYTE plans an outline you can edit, then writes it on your Mac (private, works offline).
- **Save as PDF, PowerPoint or Word** from the same document, in 4 designs (Midnight, Clean, Paper, Academic).
  PDFs get a cover, contents and page numbers; slides get real PowerPoint charts; Word files get a table of
  contents. Connected to BYTE Cloud? Switch to it in the same panel for its bigger template library.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models (Phase 1–2), tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources** that it reads and cites (Phase 2).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats (Phase 3).
- **Your files**: attach files and photos, models that see images, scanned PDFs, a knowledge base of your
  folders with cited answers, instant answers (Phase 4).
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only): cloud chat, Both side-by-side, cloud documents.
- **Documents** made on your Mac (Phase 5).

## Not in this version yet
Deeper research modes and fact-checking (v0.6), writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8),
controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final
polish (v0.12).
