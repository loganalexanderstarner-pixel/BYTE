# Phase 6 improvements — Trips and places nearby (v0.6.2)

**BYTE v0.6.2** is a Phase 6 improvement build. It has everything from v0.6.1 plus places nearby and a trip
planner. See all versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Places nearby.** "Coffee near me", "pharmacies open now in Shadyside, Pittsburgh", "best sushi in Lisbon":
  cards with distance, open-now badges, hours and a Maps button, from OpenStreetMap. For "near me", set **Your
  town** in Settings → About (BYTE never looks up where you are by itself).
- **Trip planner.** "Plan 3 days in Lisbon in May for 2 people, $1,500": a trip card with a plan for each day,
  the budget compared with yours, a packing checklist and the weather. **Save as PDF** or **Add to Calendar**.

Works with Web on; Deep and Extended research trips more thoroughly.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources**, **deep research with papers and citation styles**, **fact-checks and
  comparisons**, **places nearby and trip plans** (Phase 6).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
YouTube summaries (v0.6.3), a web agent (v0.6.4); then writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8),
controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final
polish (v0.12).
