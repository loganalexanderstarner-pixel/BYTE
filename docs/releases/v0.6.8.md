# Phase 6 improvements — Recipes in cups and spoons (v0.6.8)

**BYTE v0.6.8** has everything from v0.6.7, and recipes now use American measures. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Recipes in teaspoons, tablespoons and cups** (plus ounces, pounds and °F) by default. Recipes from websites in
  grams and millilitres are converted properly (flour, sugar and butter by their real weight per cup), and the
  metric amount stays in the note if you like to weigh.
- **US / Metric switch** on every recipe card, and a default in Settings → About → Features → Recipe measures.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Research (Phase 6)**: web search with sources, deep research with papers, fact-checks, comparisons, places,
  trips, recipes and meal plans, YouTube summaries, a web agent, reviews, prices and game hints.
- **Memory**, **your files**, **documents** (PDF, PowerPoint, Word) and **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
Writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8), controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice
and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final polish (v0.12).
