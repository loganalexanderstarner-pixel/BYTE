# Phase 7 — Study tools (v0.7.0)

**BYTE v0.7.0** starts Phase 7 (writing & learning) with study tools. It has everything from v0.6.8 too. See all
versions in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Flashcards**: "make flashcards about the French Revolution", or attach your notes and say "flashcards from
  this". Flip them in the chat, then **Study now** or **Save deck**.
- **Study panel** (🎓 in the top bar): study one card at a time (Space shows the answer, 1–4 grades it). Spaced
  repetition brings each card back right before you'd forget it. Export any deck to **Anki**.
- **Quizzes**: "quiz me on the periodic table". Multiple choice that scores itself, explains each answer, and saves
  the ones you missed as flashcards.
- **Tutor mode**: press **Tutor** in the chat box and BYTE teaches step by step, with a hint and a question for you
  each time, instead of just giving the answer.

Works offline from what BYTE knows, from your attached files, or (with Web on) from pages it reads. Switch it off in
Settings → About → Features → Study tools.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Research (Phase 6)**: web search, deep research, fact-checks, comparisons, places, trips, recipes, YouTube, a
  web agent, reviews, prices, game hints.
- **Study (Phase 7)**: flashcards, quizzes, tutor mode.
- **Memory**, **your files**, **documents** (PDF, PowerPoint, Word) and **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
The writing studio (v0.7.1), translate, a job tracker and custom assistants (later v0.7.x), a tuning panel (v0.8),
controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and polish (v0.12).
