# Phase 6 — Deep research (v0.6.0)

**BYTE v0.6.0** is the first Phase 6 build. It has everything from Phases 1–5 plus deep research. See all
versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Deep and Extended research.** Pick **Deep** or **Extended** in the chat box and ask about anything in the
  world. BYTE plans several searches from different angles, reads 12 pages (Deep) or 24 (Extended), keeps the
  most relevant passages, and writes a sectioned report with a TL;DR and numbered citations. Extended also
  looks for what's still missing and searches again. You can watch every step in the activity list.
- **Research papers.** Questions about studies, evidence, health or science also search published papers
  (Crossref, Europe PMC, arXiv; no account needed). Paper sources show their year and journal.
- **Confidence line.** Research answers end with **Verified**, **Likely** or **Unsure**, with the reason.
- **Cite button.** Under an answer's sources: every source in APA, MLA, Chicago, Harvard, IEEE or BibTeX,
  with Copy and Copy all.

Tip: download the search-by-meaning model (Settings → Knowledge base) and research picks passages by meaning,
not just matching words.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models (Phase 1–2), tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources** that it reads and cites (Phase 2), and **deep research with papers** (Phase 6).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats (Phase 3).
- **Your files**: attach files and photos, models that see images, scanned PDFs, a knowledge base of your
  folders with cited answers, instant answers (Phase 4).
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word (Phase 5).
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only): cloud chat, Both side-by-side, cloud documents.
