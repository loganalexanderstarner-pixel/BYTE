## What BYTE can do
- **Chat on your Mac's GPU**, even offline, with ⚡ Fast, 🎚 Auto, 🔭 Deep and 🚀 Extended modes. Setup picks the
  best model for your Mac and measures its real speed.
- **Research with sources:** web search and reading with numbered citations, deep research with papers,
  fact-checks, comparisons, reviews, prices, trip plans, places nearby and YouTube summaries. No API keys.
- **Your files:** PDFs, Office files, scans and photos; your folders as a searchable knowledge base; notes, a web
  clipper, mind maps and a brainstorm board.
- **Documents:** PDF reports, Word documents and PowerPoint slides (beta) with themes and charts.
- **Writing and learning:** a writing studio, long-form writer, "write like me", translation, flashcards, quizzes
  and a tutor.
- **Your Mac, with your OK:** reminders, calendar, notes, Mail drafts, texts you send from an editable card, a
  Messages inbox with Draft a reply, music, settings, files, storage cleanup and an uninstaller, most with Undo.
- **Everyday helpers:** to-dos, schedules, a daily briefing, news feeds, page and price watchers, automations,
  Shortcuts, trackers and Notion, Obsidian and calendar connectors.
- **Voice:** dictation, "Hey BYTE", spoken answers in thousands of free natural voices, hands-free talking and
  transcripts with speaker labels.
- **Private by design:** an offline switch, a Touch ID lock, an activity log, kids mode, encrypted iCloud backups,
  auto-delete and erase everything.
- **Yours:** 20 themes or your own, personality settings, Quick Ask (⌥Space), a menu-bar icon, ⌘K, an offline help
  center, and signed one-click updates.
- **Optional BYTE Cloud** (invite only) for bigger models, with your Mac as the fallback.

## Good to know
- A model that fits on a 16 GB Mac is very good at everyday work but reasons less deeply than the biggest online
  assistants.
- BYTE isn't signed by Apple, so the first launch needs "Open Anyway" (or install with Homebrew). If an update says
  the disk is read-only, run `xattr -cr /Applications/BYTE.app` once.
- PowerPoint output is still beta.
