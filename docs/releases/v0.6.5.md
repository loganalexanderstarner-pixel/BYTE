# Phase 6 improvements — YouTube summaries (v0.6.5)

**BYTE v0.6.5** is a Phase 6 improvement build. It has everything from v0.6.4 plus YouTube summaries and
questions about videos. See all versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Summarize a YouTube video**: paste a link. You get a card with the thumbnail, a TL;DR, key points and
  chapters; every timestamp opens the video at that moment. Copy it or save it as a PDF.
- **Ask about a video**: "What does he say about X in <link>?", or ask a follow-up in the same chat. BYTE answers
  from the transcript and links the exact moments.

Needs Web on (Auto or Always). Works with videos that have captions (most do); others can't be read yet.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources** (Off/Auto/Always), **deep research with papers and citation styles**,
  **fact-checks and comparisons**, **places nearby and trip plans**, **recipes and meal plans**, **YouTube
  summaries** (Phase 6).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
A web agent (v0.6.6); then writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8), controlling Mac apps (v0.9),
automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final polish (v0.12).
