# Phase 11 improvements — Personality, help, ideas and the brainstorm board (v0.11.7)

**BYTE v0.11.7** completes **Phase 11 (input and windows)**. It adds a personality you can tune, an offline help
center, fresh ideas on the home screen, and a brainstorm board. It has everything from v0.11.6 too. See all versions
in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Personality** (Settings → About):
  - five sliders: warmth, length, humor, formality and opinions;
  - presets: Balanced (BYTE's usual voice), Friendly, Concise, Professional, Teacher and Coach;
  - **Try it** asks a sample question in a private chat;
  - it applies on your Mac and with BYTE Cloud.
- **Help center** (the lifebuoy button, ⌘?, or ⌘K → Help):
  - 14 short articles that work offline, with search and links straight to the right setting;
  - **Ideas to try**: a list of things to ask, showing only the ones that work with your settings.
- **Ask BYTE about BYTE:** questions like "how do I change BYTE's voice?" are answered from the help articles, so
  even small models get them right.
- **Fresh ideas on the home screen:** 4 suggestions that change daily and match the features you have on, plus
  **More ideas to try**.
- **Brainstorm board** (the shapes button, or ⌘K → Brainstorm board):
  - sticky notes you drag, double-click to edit and recolor;
  - **Add ideas**: BYTE adds 5 new ones on your topic;
  - **Expand this**: follow-ups for the selected sticky;
  - **Group into themes**: sorts everything into labelled columns;
  - turn the board into a **mind map** or **save it as a note**;
  - boards are saved on your Mac.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts, voice input,
  speaker labels, spoken answers, hands-free talking, “Hey BYTE”, a catalog of natural voices, notes, a web clipper,
  mind maps, a brainstorm board, personality and help.
