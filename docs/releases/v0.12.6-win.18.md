# Windows test build 18: edit photos, a gallery, posters and more (v0.12.6-win.18)

**A test build of BYTE for Windows 10 and 11.** Not the finished app: tell us what you find. The Mac app is the same as v0.12.6.

## Installing
Download `BYTE_0.12.6-win.18_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows will say it "protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It updates the previous test build in place.

## New in Images (Settings → Images). Every part has its own switch, off until you turn it on, and none work in kids mode.
- **Edit photos.** Change a photo with a sentence (remove a person, restore an old photo, colorize, change the lighting, change the style, change the words on a sign), make it 2 or 4 times bigger, crop it, add film grain or motion blur, or give it the camera finish. A before and after slider shows what changed, and your original is never touched. Sentence edits use an editing model from the catalog: Qwen-Image Edit (the strongest), FLUX.1 Kontext, or the small, quick FLUX.2 klein.
- **Gallery.** Everything you made or edited, with what you asked for, which model made it and how long it took. Save, copy out, edit or delete a picture.
- **Compare models.** Make the same picture with several models, one after another with the same seed, and see them side by side.
- **Prompt helper.** Turns a short idea into a fuller description (as a photo, painting, poster or cartoon). Uses BYTE's chat model when it is running.
- **Poem and quote posters.** Put a poem on a picture. BYTE finds the quiet part of the picture, sets the words there and keeps your line breaks. Five lettering styles: typewriter, elegant, neon sign, dripping paint and hand lettered. Draw art for the poem or pick a picture from your gallery.
- **Draw from chat and Hey BYTE.** Say or type "draw me a lighthouse" and BYTE makes it with your best downloaded model. Never in private chats.
- **More models.** The catalog is now 169 models and upscalers, including the editing models, FLUX.2 klein and more community Stable Diffusion models.
- **Smarter memory use.** Big models keep room on your graphics card so they no longer run out of video memory while BYTE's chat model is loaded.

## Also in this build
- **Quiet hours** (Settings → Voice and Hey BYTE, off by default). Pick a start and end time, for example 22:00 to 07:00. In that window "Hey BYTE" is switched off (the listening pill says so) and BYTE says nothing on its own. Typing to BYTE and the talk key still work.
- **The Settings search box** no longer sticks out past the left menu.
- Help and the Tutorial have new pages for all of this.

## Good to know
- The first picture after a restart can take a few minutes while the model is read from disk. Later ones are much quicker.
- Editing with a sentence on a big model (Qwen-Image Edit) takes about 3 minutes on an RTX 5080 while BYTE's chat model is loaded. FLUX.2 klein is much quicker.

## Please try
- Turn on **Edit photos**, download **FLUX.2 klein 4B** (or Qwen-Image Edit 2511) and change a photo with a sentence.
- Turn on **Poem and quote posters** and try each lettering style.
- Turn on **Draw from chat and Hey BYTE** and say "draw me …".
- Tell us if a model you download does not run on your graphics card.
