# Phase 12 improvements — Texting from BYTE, and a Messages inbox (v0.12.6)

**BYTE v0.12.6** sends your texts and helps you answer the ones you get. See all versions in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Texts send from BYTE:** "text Mom I'm on my way" shows a card with the text in a box you can edit. Press
  **Send** and it goes through Messages (iMessage, or SMS through your iPhone). Nothing is sent until you press
  Send, and a sent text can't be taken back from BYTE.
- **Writing tools on every text:** **Fix grammar & punctuation**, **Rephrase** in a tone (**Formal**, **Friendly**,
  **Fun**, **Sympathetic**, **Shorter**), **Ideas** with a few versions to pick from, and **Undo**.
- **Messages inbox** (Settings → Privacy, off until you turn it on): texts you receive show up in BYTE, with a
  banner and a notification. **Draft a reply** writes an answer from the conversation (tell it what you want to say
  if you like), and **Reply** lets you write your own. It needs Full Disk Access, reads your messages on this Mac
  only, read-only, and stays off in kids mode and while BYTE is locked.

- **Stories stay stories:** asking for a story, poem, song or joke never starts a Mac action, even when the story
  mentions texting, a reminder or a time. (Add "…and save it to my notes" when you do want that.)

## What BYTE can do in this version
Everything in v0.12.5, plus texting and the Messages inbox.
