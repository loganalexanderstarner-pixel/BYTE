# Phase 11 improvements — BYTE's own natural voices (v0.11.4)

**BYTE v0.11.4** gives BYTE natural, human-sounding voices instead of the Mac's robotic one: 28 to choose from,
free and open source, made on your Mac. BYTE starts talking while an answer is still being written, and the
sentences flow into each other with no pauses in between. It also brings v0.11.3 (spoken answers, Talk mode and “Hey BYTE”), which has no separate download: its
Mac test caught a “Hey BYTE” bug that is fixed here. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **BYTE's voices** (Settings → Models → Voice): a one-time 350 MB download (Kokoro, free and open source).
  - 28 English voices: American and British, female and male. Heart is the default; try Michael, Emma or George.
  - **Try it** plays a sample in the voice you picked.
  - Everything is made on your Mac. No account, no subscription, nothing sent anywhere.
- **Smooth speech**, like the big assistants:
  - BYTE starts speaking after the first sentence is written, not after the whole answer.
  - One continuous audio stream plays the sentences back to back, so there's no talk-pause-talk.
  - It works for the 🔊 button, "Read answers aloud" and Talk mode. Stop and Esc still stop it at once.
- Without the download, BYTE keeps using the Mac's own voice, as before.
- **From v0.11.3:** 🔊 on every answer, Read answers aloud, Talk with BYTE (hands-free), and “Hey BYTE”.
- **Fixed:** “Hey BYTE” said quickly (about half a second) could be missed; it's heard now.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts, voice input,
  speaker labels, spoken answers, hands-free talking, “Hey BYTE”, and now BYTE's own natural voices.

## Not in this version yet
- A catalog of more voices, more human speech, and “Hey BYTE” answering out loud: v0.11.5.
- Notes, the web clipper and mind maps: v0.11.6.
- The brainstorm board, the help center, example prompts and personality settings: v0.11.7.
- BYTE's voices, reading aloud and “Hey BYTE” need a Mac for now.
