# Phase 12 improvements — Your own themes, smoother motion and accessibility (v0.12.2)

**BYTE v0.12.2** ships inside v0.12.3 (no separate download). It lets you design your own theme, makes BYTE's motion smoother (and easy to turn down), and checks
that every button can be read by VoiceOver. It has everything from v0.12.1 too. See all versions in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Make your own theme** (Settings → Appearance):
  - start from any of the 20 themes and pick five colors: background, panels, lines, text and accent;
  - a live preview, and BYTE warns you if text, labels or buttons would be hard to read;
  - keep up to 10 of your own;
  - **Export** and **Import** `.bytetheme` files to share them.
- **Smoother motion:** dialogs and panels spring in, and BYTE's logo glows while it's thinking.
- **Reduce motion** (Settings → Appearance): Auto follows macOS's setting, or Reduce turns animations down
  everywhere.
- **Sounds** (optional, off by default): a soft chime when an answer is ready, made by BYTE (no sound files).
- **Accessibility:** every button has a name VoiceOver can read, and a new check keeps it that way.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, voice input, spoken answers,
  “Hey BYTE”, 2,000+ natural voices, notes, a web clipper, mind maps, a brainstorm board, personality and help.
- **Privacy:** an offline switch, a Touch ID lock, an activity log, kids mode, encrypted backups and erasing.
- **Looks:** 20 themes plus your own, smooth motion, and VoiceOver-friendly controls.
