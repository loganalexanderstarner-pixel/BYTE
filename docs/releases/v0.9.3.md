# Phase 9 — Files and a terminal helper (v0.9.3)

**BYTE v0.9.3** completes Phase 9 (Mac control): BYTE now finds and tidies your files, converts photos, answers
questions about what you've selected in Finder, and runs Terminal commands for you after you OK them. It has
everything from v0.9.2 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Find files**: "find my tax return pdf", "where's my resume?". BYTE searches your Mac with Spotlight and lists
  the likeliest files with their folders.
- **Tidy a folder**: "organize my Downloads" (by kind: Images, Documents, Installers…) or "tidy my desktop by month".
  - You see where everything goes before anything moves.
  - Only loose files move; folders and files still downloading are left alone. Nothing is deleted or overwritten.
  - **Undo** puts everything back.
- **Photos**: select photos in Finder and say "convert the selected photos to jpg" or "make the selected photos
  smaller". New copies are saved next to the originals, which stay as they are; **Undo** removes the copies.
- **Ask about files you selected in Finder**: "summarize the files I selected in Finder".
- **Terminal helper**: "run a command to show how much disk space is free", "use the terminal to see what's using
  port 3000".
  - BYTE shows the command and what it does, and runs it only when you press **Do it**, then explains the result.
  - Commands that could harm your Mac (sudo, erasing disks, deleting everything, running downloaded scripts) are
    refused, whatever the model suggests.
- Also: v0.9.1's **Mail and Messages** are in this build too (v0.9.1 shipped inside v0.9.2).

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control (Phase 9, complete)**: Reminders, Calendar, Notes, Music, Shortcuts, Safari, system settings, Mail,
  Messages, selected text in any app, clipboard history, files and Terminal.

## Not in this version yet
Automations and upkeep (v0.10: storage cleanup, scheduled tasks, daily briefing), voice and quick-ask windows plus a
`byte://` link for other apps (v0.11), Touch ID and polish (v0.12). Making new Shortcuts for you is planned with the
automations.
