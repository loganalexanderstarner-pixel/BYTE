# Phase 11 improvements — Voice input (v0.11.1)

**BYTE v0.11.1** lets you talk instead of typing, and turns recordings into text BYTE can work with. Speech is
turned into text on your Mac by BYTE's own voice engine; your voice never leaves the Mac. It has everything from
v0.11.0 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **🎤 in the message box**: click it, talk, and click again to stop. What you said appears in the box, so you can
  check it and press Enter. **Holding Space** in an empty box does the same while you hold it. This works in the
  main window and in Quick Ask (⌥Space).
- **Speech models**, a one-time download offered the first time you press 🎤:
  - **Best (any language)**, 574 MB: very accurate, about 100 languages;
  - **Quick (English)**, 148 MB: smaller, good for short dictation.
- **Recordings become transcripts**: attach or drop an audio file (WAV, MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG and more, up to 500 MB)
  and BYTE reads it as a transcript. Then ask "summarize this meeting" or "what were the action items?".
- **Settings → Models → Voice**: turn voice input on or off, pick or delete speech models, and choose the spoken
  language ("Detect it" by default).
- The first time, macOS asks you to allow the microphone.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice (new):** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, custom shortcuts and voice
  input.

## Not in this version yet
- Speaker labels in transcripts ("who said what"), and summaries of videos without captions: v0.11.2.
- BYTE speaking its answers, hands-free conversation and "Hey BYTE": v0.11.3.
- Notes, the web clipper and mind maps: v0.11.4.
