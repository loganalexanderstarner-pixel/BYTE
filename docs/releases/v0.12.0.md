# Phase 12 — Offline switch, Touch ID lock and a Privacy tab (v0.12.0)

**BYTE v0.12.0** starts **Phase 12 (privacy and polish)**, the last phase before v1.0. You can now take BYTE fully
offline with one switch, lock it with Touch ID, and see everything it did for you. It has everything from v0.11.7
too. See all versions in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Offline switch** (Settings → Privacy, the menu-bar icon, or ⌘K → Go offline):
  - nothing leaves your Mac: no web search, no BYTE Cloud, no downloads, no background checks;
  - it's enforced for every connection BYTE makes, even through a proxy, so no feature can slip past it;
  - chats, voices, your files and notes keep working with the model on your Mac;
  - an **Offline** pill at the top shows it's on; click it to go back online.
- **Lock BYTE with Touch ID** (Settings → Privacy):
  - BYTE asks for Touch ID, or your Mac's password, when it opens and after it sits unused (5, 15 or 60 minutes,
    or only when it opens);
  - while locked, chats, notes, memories and the activity log stay hidden, and Quick Ask asks too;
  - **Lock now** is in ⌘K and the menu-bar icon.
- **Privacy tab** (Settings → Privacy):
  - what can reach the internet, and whether each part is on;
  - the Mac permissions BYTE may use (Microphone, Accessibility, Automation, Calendars, Reminders,
    Notifications), what each is for, whether it's allowed, and a button to open it in System Settings;
  - **Activity**: everything BYTE did for you (searches, pages read, Mac actions, terminal commands, files,
    connectors, automations), with search, filters, Copy as JSON and Clear. It's kept on your Mac only.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, connectors and
  dashboards.
- **Windows, keys and voice:** Quick Ask, the menu-bar icon, the command palette, voice input, spoken answers,
  “Hey BYTE”, 2,000+ natural voices, notes, a web clipper, mind maps, a brainstorm board, personality and help.
- **Privacy:** an offline switch, a Touch ID lock, and a full activity log.

## Not in this version yet

- The lock hides BYTE's data inside the app. Your chats are already encrypted on your Mac, but tying the encryption
