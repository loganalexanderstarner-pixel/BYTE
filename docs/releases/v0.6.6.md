# Phase 6 — Web agent (v0.6.6)

**BYTE v0.6.6** adds the web agent: BYTE can use a browser for you. It has everything from v0.6.5 too. See all
versions and what each one has in [docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **BYTE uses a browser for you**: "Go to carnegielibrary.org and find when the Squirrel Hill branch opens",
  "fill out the contact form on example.com", "download the manual from <link>", "save this page as a PDF:
  <link>", or press **Agent** in the chat box. It opens sites, clicks, types into forms, picks options,
  scrolls and goes back, and every step shows as it happens.
- **It always asks first**: before submitting, buying, booking, sending, deleting or downloading, a card shows
  the site and everything it filled in. Nothing happens until you press **Submit** (or **Download**). If you say
  no, it stops.
- **It never types passwords or card numbers.** For logins, payments or CAPTCHAs, press **Show browser** and do
  that part yourself.
- **Downloads and saved pages** go to Downloads/BYTE: files up to 200 MB, and pages as a whole-page PDF, a
  picture or a web archive.

Private: the browser keeps nothing and closes when the answer ends. Public websites only. Needs Web on (Auto or
Always); switch it off in Settings → About → Features. Works best with a bigger model (8B or more): small models
may stop early.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** with a built-in engine and hundreds of downloadable models, tuned per Mac.
- **Web search with sources** (Off/Auto/Always), **deep research with papers and citation styles**,
  **fact-checks and comparisons**, **places nearby and trip plans**, **recipes and meal plans**, **YouTube
  summaries**, and now **a web agent** (Phase 6).
- **Memory**: saved, searchable, encrypted chats, projects, profiles, private chats.
- **Your files**: attachments, photos, scans, a knowledge base of your folders.
- **Documents** made on your Mac: PDF, PowerPoint and Word.
- **BYTE Cloud** (invite only).

## Not in this version yet
Writing and study tools (v0.7), a tuning panel (v0.8), controlling Mac apps (v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice
and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and final polish (v0.12).
