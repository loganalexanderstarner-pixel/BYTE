# Windows test build 17: make pictures on your PC (v0.12.6-win.17)

**A test build of BYTE for Windows 10 and 11.** Not the finished app: tell us what you find. The Mac app is the same as v0.12.6.

## Installing
Download `BYTE_0.12.6-win.17_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows will say it "protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It updates the previous test build in place.

## New: Images (Settings → Images)
- **Make pictures from words, on your own PC.** Nothing leaves it. Off until you turn on **Make pictures**; off in kids mode.
- **A catalog of 124 free image models** with every quant version (for example Q4 or Q8) and 17 upscalers. Each version says whether it fits your video memory, overflows into RAM (and by how much), or is too big.
- **Models are never hidden by a filter.** Cards show what each is good at. Pick what you want to do, then a style. **Realism** has real-life looks: portraits, street, landscapes, animals, product photos, food, buildings, movie-still and film look.
- **The picture engine downloads once**, on request, and is checked against a fixed checksum (NVIDIA CUDA, any graphics card with Vulkan, or processor only).
- **Switches, all off until you turn them on:** Realistic people (with a plain disclaimer), Detail pass, Enlarge each picture (pick an upscaler; 4xNomosWebPhoto keeps real skin texture), Camera finish (grain and colour like a camera), Where the model lives (Auto, video memory only, RAM only), Keep my pictures, Remove settings from picture files.
- **First-run tutorial button.** The last startup page has **Show me around**: a short, friendly tour of everything. Leave it any time.
- **Help and Tutorial** have new pages for all of this.

## Also in this build
- The same fixes and features as build 16: settings search suggestions, microphone and speaker choice, Test my microphone.

## Please try
- Turn on **Make pictures**, download the engine, then download **Z-Image Turbo** and make a picture.
- Turn on **Detail pass**, **Enlarge** and **Camera finish** and compare.
- Tell us if a model you download does not run on your graphics card.
