# Phase 10 — Mac upkeep (v0.10.0)

**BYTE v0.10.0** starts Phase 10 (upkeep & automation): BYTE shows what's filling your disk and clears the safe
parts, explains why your Mac is slow or the battery drains, runs a health check, uninstalls apps with their
leftover files, and manages what opens at login. It has everything from v0.9.3 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Storage**: "what's taking up space?", "free up space", "find duplicate files", "show my biggest files".
  - A chart of your biggest folders, how full the disk is, and what could safely go: old installers in Downloads,
    exact duplicate files (the oldest copy is kept), and Xcode and developer caches, each with why it's safe.
  - **Move to Trash** on the card asks once more, then moves things to the Trash through Finder. **Undo** (or
    Finder's Put Back) restores them. BYTE never empties the Trash and never touches macOS itself, your Library
    (apart from caches) or files inside app and Photos libraries.
- **Why is my Mac slow? / What's draining my battery?**: memory, processor, what keeps the Mac awake, battery
  health and when it last restarted, with the busiest programs and a **Quit** button for apps.
- **Check my Mac**: storage, memory, battery health, Time Machine, FileVault, firewall and macOS version, worst first,
  each with a button that opens the right System Settings page.
- **Uninstall apps**: "uninstall Zoom" finds the app and its files in your Library (settings, caches, containers),
  shows them with sizes, and after your OK quits the app and moves everything to the Trash. **Undo** puts it back.
  macOS's own apps and BYTE itself are refused.
- **Login items**: "what opens at login?" lists them (plus background helpers); "stop Spotify from opening at
  login" removes it after your OK, with **Undo**.
- Settings → Features → **Mac upkeep** switches it all off.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control**: Reminders, Calendar, Notes, Music, Shortcuts, Safari, system settings, Mail, Messages, selected
  text in any app, clipboard history, files and Terminal.
- **Mac upkeep (new)**: storage clean-up, slowness and battery help, health check, uninstalling, login items.
