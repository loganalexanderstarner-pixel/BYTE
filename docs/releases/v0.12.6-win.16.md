# Windows test build 16: search suggestions, microphone and speaker choice (v0.12.6-win.16)

**A test build of BYTE for Windows 10 and 11.** Not the finished app: tell us what you find. The Mac app is the same as v0.12.6.

## Installing
Download `BYTE_0.12.6-win.16_x64-setup.exe` below and run it. Windows will say it "protected your PC": choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**. It updates the previous test build in place.

## New since build 15
- **Settings search suggests as you type.** Type "a" in the Settings search box and the settings starting with "a" drop down under it. Arrow keys, Enter or a click jump to the setting.
- **Choose your microphone and speakers** under Voice and Hey BYTE. They start on "System default". Changing the microphone restarts Hey BYTE on it by itself.
- **Test my microphone.** Press Test, say "Hey BYTE", and BYTE shows what it heard and whether that would have woken it. Nothing is kept.
- **New help article:** Choosing your microphone and speakers.

## Please try
- Pick a different microphone, press Test, and say "Hey BYTE" at normal volume and from across the room.
- Say "Hey BYTE" while a spoken answer is playing, and ask a question with a follow-up.
