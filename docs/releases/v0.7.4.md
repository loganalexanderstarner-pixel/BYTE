# Phase 7 — Writing studio (v0.7.4)

**BYTE v0.7.4** adds the writing studio: rewrite, shorten, expand, change the tone of, or fix the grammar of anything
you write, and see exactly what changed before you use it. It has everything from v0.7.3 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Writing studio** (✍️ in the top bar, or the pen under any answer).
  - Paste an email, essay or post, then pick **Rewrite**, **Shorten**, **Expand**, **Fix grammar**, or a **Tone**
    (Friendly, Formal, Confident, Simple, Persuasive).
  - The new version appears next to yours with the changed words highlighted. **Use this** puts it in; **Copy** and
    **Discard** do what they say.
  - Select a sentence or paragraph to change only that part.
  - It works with the model on your Mac, or your BYTE cloud when no model is loaded.
- **"A better model fits your Mac."** When you're on a small model and your Mac can comfortably run a much better
  one, BYTE tells you once.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research (Phase 6)**: web search, deep research, fact-checks, comparisons, places, trips, recipes, YouTube, a
  web agent, reviews, prices, game hints.
- **Study and writing (Phase 7)**: flashcards, quizzes, tutor mode, the writing studio.
- **Memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents** (PDF, PowerPoint, Word).

## Not in this version yet
Translate (v0.7.5), a job tracker and custom assistants (later v0.7.x), a tuning panel (v0.8), controlling Mac apps
(v0.9), automations (v0.10), voice and quick-ask windows (v0.11), Touch ID and polish (v0.12). Small models (around
1B) fix spelling well but can miss grammar like "their/they're"; bigger models catch it.
