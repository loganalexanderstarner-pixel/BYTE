# Phase 10 — Dashboards; Phase 10 complete (v0.10.6)

**BYTE v0.10.6** opens to a home screen that shows your day at a glance. It also adds a library of every answer
BYTE researched for you and a private look at how you use BYTE. This completes Phase 10: Mac upkeep, tasks and
schedules, feeds and watchers, automations, trackers, connectors and dashboards. It has everything from v0.10.5
too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **The command-deck home**: a new chat opens to live tiles above the usual suggestions. Each tile shows only when
  it has something to show:
  - **Today:** your events, from the Mac Calendar and your calendar links.
  - **To-dos:** what's due and overdue.
  - **Coming up:** bills, birthdays and maintenance in the next two weeks.
  - **News and watched pages:** price drops, page changes and new stories.
  - **Next automation:** when your next one runs.
  - **Research library:** how many chats have sources.
  - Clicking a tile asks BYTE about it, for example "what's coming up?" or "give me my briefing".
- **Research library**: every chat whose answers cite sources (web research, papers, your files), newest first,
  with search. It opens from its home tile.
- **Your usage** (Settings → About): questions this week, chats, answers with sources, average speed, the modes you
  use most, and a 14-day chart. It's counted from the chats on this Mac and never sent anywhere.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**.
- **Everyday helpers:** tasks and schedules, feeds and watchers, automations and Shortcuts, trackers, and
  connectors (Obsidian, Notion, calendar links).
- **Dashboards (new):** a live home screen, the research library, and usage stats.

## Not in this version yet
Phase 11 (input and windows) comes next: voice, a Quick Ask window, a menu-bar mini chat, a command palette, notes,
mind maps. Google, Dropbox, OneDrive and Spotify connectors wait until BYTE is registered with them.
