# Phase 10 improvements — Trackers (v0.10.4)

**BYTE v0.10.4** keeps track of the things that are easy to forget: packages on the way, bills and subscriptions,
birthdays and other yearly dates, and car and home maintenance. You get a notification ahead of time, and it's all
kept on your Mac. It has everything from v0.10.3 too. See all versions in
[docs/VERSIONS.md](https://github.com/loganalexanderstarner-pixel/BYTE/blob/claude/new-session-tu1a5x/docs/VERSIONS.md).

## New in this version
- **Bills and subscriptions**: "add Netflix $15.49 a month on the 12th", "my rent is $1,500 a month due on the 1st",
  "Amazon Prime costs $139 a year".
  - "What subscriptions do I have?" lists them with what they cost per month and per year. BYTE adds these up
    itself, so the totals are exact.
  - A notification 3 days before each payment. "I cancelled Netflix" stops tracking it.
- **Birthdays and yearly dates**: "Sam's birthday is March 3", "our anniversary is June 12", "Mom's birthday is on
  5/14, budget $80".
  - A notification 2 weeks ahead, with any gift ideas you saved.
  - "Add AirPods to Sam's gift ideas" saves an idea. "Gift ideas for Sam" suggests some, based on what BYTE knows.
- **Car and home maintenance**: "change the furnace filter every 3 months", "track oil changes every 6 months".
  - A notification a week before it's due. "I changed the oil today" marks it done, and the next date moves on.
- **Packages**: "track 1Z999AA10123456784 for my new running shoes, arriving friday".
  - BYTE recognizes UPS, USPS, FedEx, DHL and Amazon numbers and links to the carrier's own tracking page.
  - It doesn't read the delivery status itself: carriers block automated visits, and a guessed status would be
    worse than none. "My running shoes arrived" marks the package delivered.
- "What's coming up?" lists everything in the next 30 days.
- **The ✅ panel has a Trackers section**, with tabs for Packages, Bills, Dates and Maintenance. You can add,
  mark done or delete there, and it shows your monthly and yearly total for bills.
- Switch trackers off in Settings → Features → **Trackers**.

## What BYTE can do in this version
- **Chat on your Mac** or with **BYTE Cloud** (invite only), with cards, on many kinds of models.
- **Research**, **writing and learning**, **speed and tuning**, **memory**, **your files and photos**, **documents**.
- **Mac control** and **Mac upkeep**: Apple apps, Mail, Messages, selected text, clipboard, files, Terminal, storage
  clean-up, health checks, uninstalling, login items.
- **Tasks, schedules, feeds, watchers and automations**: to-dos, a daily briefing, news digests, price alerts, routines,
  and Shortcuts.
- **Trackers (new)**: packages, bills and subscriptions, birthdays, and maintenance.

## Not in this version yet
Connectors (Google, Notion, Obsidian and more; v0.10.5) and dashboards (v0.10.6). BYTE doesn't read package status
or bank statements. You tell it about bills, and nothing is sent anywhere.
