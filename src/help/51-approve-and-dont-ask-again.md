When BYTE asks before a step on your PC, the card has two buttons: **Approve**, and **Approve and don't ask again**. The second one saves a rule for **that kind of step, in that program, for you**, for example "press Send buttons in Outlook".

## See and remove rules
[Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control) → **Things BYTE no longer asks about** lists every rule with a remove button, and **Remove all**.

## What can never be remembered
Payments (Buy, Pay, Place order, Checkout), opening program files and scripts (.exe, .bat, .ps1, installers), dangerous key combinations (like Win+R) and shutting down. They always ask or are refused, whatever you choose, and **Stop** always works.

## How much BYTE asks
**How much BYTE asks** has three choices:
- **Ask for risky steps:** a card before anything risky. This is the default.
- **Ask only about things outside a run contract:** steps inside the programs and folders you named go ahead; the rest wait for you.
- **Never ask, just do it:** no cards at all. The refusals above still hold.
