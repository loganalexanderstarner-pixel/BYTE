# Tasks, schedules and automations

- The ✅ button holds **to-dos**, **schedules**, **trackers** (packages, bills, birthdays) and **automations**.
- Say "remind me to…", "every weekday at 8am give me my briefing", "follow theverge.com", or "tell me when this drops below $50".
- **Automations** chain steps: "research X, then write a summary and save it to a file". Add one to Shortcuts with the link button.
- Schedules run while BYTE is open (it can keep running in the menu bar when you close the window).
