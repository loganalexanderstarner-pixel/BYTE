# Modes, web search and research

- **Fast** answers briefly. **Auto** matches the question. **Deep** thinks longer and researches. **Extended** writes full reports with many sources.
- **Web** (under the message box) is **Off**, **Auto** (searches when a question needs fresh facts) or **Always**.
- Answers that use the web cite sources like **[1]**; click one to open it. **Cite** copies a reference in APA, MLA and other styles.
- Ask to **fact-check** a claim, **compare** products, **plan a trip**, find **places near you**, summarize a **YouTube** video, or read **reviews and prices**: BYTE shows a card for each.
