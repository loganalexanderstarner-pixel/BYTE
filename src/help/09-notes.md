# Notes, web clipper and mind maps

- **📝 Notes:** Markdown notes with folders and tags, saved as plain files in Documents/BYTE/Notes. They open in any editor.
- **📝 under an answer** saves it as a note.
- **Web clipper:** drag **Clip to BYTE** from [Settings → About](byte-setting:about) to your bookmarks bar. Click it on any page to save the page to your notes.
- **Mind map:** the branch button under a longer answer (or in a note) draws it as a map. Fold branches, then export PNG or SVG.
- **Brainstorm board:** the board button opens a canvas of sticky notes. BYTE can add ideas, group them into themes and turn them into an outline.
