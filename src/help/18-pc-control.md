PC control is **off until you turn it on**: [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control) → **Let BYTE use this PC for you**. When it is on, BYTE reads the window in front as a list of buttons and boxes, then clicks, types and presses keys for you.

## Commands or conversation
**How BYTE handles commands** decides when BYTE acts:
- **Smart** (default): acts on clear commands ("set the volume to 30", "open Spotify") and only talks about everything else ("how do I turn off the Wi-Fi?").
- **Always:** anything that matches a command runs.
- **Chat only:** BYTE acts only when your message starts with **do:**, for example "do: set the volume to 30".
It works the same typed or spoken.

## Try it safely
1. Turn on PC control.
2. Say or type "open Notepad".
3. Watch the bar at the top: it says BYTE is using your PC and has a **Stop** button.

## What it can do
Open and use programs through their windows, adjust sound, brightness and Wi-Fi, lock the PC, take a screenshot, mute the microphone and control media. It shows an **approval card** before anything risky (shutting down, closing windows, opening files, links that are not websites).

## Limits you choose
- **How long one run may take**, **Steps in one plan** and **Tries to re-plan** (each has an Unlimited option).
- **Ask before risky steps** (on). **Never ask, just do it** turns the cards off after a confirmation; program files and dangerous key presses stay refused.
- **Programs BYTE may use without asking:** your own list.
- **Stop** (the button on the bar) ends a run before its next step.
- Everything BYTE did is under **What BYTE did**. Typed text is never stored there.

## The helper model
**Model that does the controlling** is your chat model or a model you pick from a list of capable ones. **Keep the game fast** keeps it in system memory while a game runs, so your graphics card stays free.

## Routines
Turn on **Voice routines** to run several steps with one phrase, like "game time". Add, remove and schedule them in the routines menu.

## Find files by voice (off by default)
Turn on **Find files by voice** and choose the folders BYTE may search. Say "find my tax return" and BYTE opens File Explorer with the file selected. It searches only your chosen folders.

## How's my PC?
Ask "how's my PC?" for a short spoken status of memory, disk, processor and graphics card.
