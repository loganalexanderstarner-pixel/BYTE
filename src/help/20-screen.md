Everything here is **off until you turn it on**, in [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control) → **Looking at your screen**. BYTE never looks unless you ask, apart from the replay, which has its own switch and a visible badge.

## Ask about the screen
Turn on **Ask about what's on my screen**. Then ask "what do you think of this page?" or "explain this error". BYTE captures the window in front once, reads its text and answers. In a private chat it refuses and captures nothing. If you picked BYTE Cloud for the answer, only words describing the picture are sent, never the picture.

## The replay
Turn on **Remember the last seconds of my screen** and BYTE keeps the last 30 seconds (you can choose 5 to 120).
- It lives **in memory only**: never saved, never sent, gone when you quit, switch it off or open a private chat.
- It stops the moment a protected window is in front (password managers, banking, private browser windows). You can edit **Never look at**.
- A badge shows while it is recording, and Windows draws its own border around a window being captured.
- Ask "where did I go wrong?" after a round. BYTE looks at a few still pictures, so it can describe what happened and the likely mistake, but it cannot judge aim or reaction time.

Windowed and borderless games work; full-screen exclusive games may give a black picture, which BYTE reports instead of guessing.

## Game advisor and Read it to me
- **Game advisor:** ask for advice about what is on screen in a game; it does not know any game's rules, so it says when it cannot tell.
- **Read it to me:** BYTE reads your selection or the window's text aloud.
