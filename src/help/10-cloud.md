# BYTE Cloud

BYTE Cloud is an optional, invite-only server with bigger models.

<!-- mac -->
- Make an account on the BYTE website (open your invite link). Signed in, open **Settings** and scroll to the **bottom** for your key. Paste it in [Settings → Account and cloud](byte-setting:cloud); BYTE keeps it in your Mac's Keychain.
<!-- win -->
- Make an account on the BYTE website (open your invite link). Signed in, open **Settings** and scroll to the **bottom** for your key. Paste it in [Settings → Account and cloud](byte-setting:cloud); BYTE keeps it in Windows Credential Manager.
<!-- linux -->
- Make an account on the BYTE website (open your invite link). Signed in, open **Settings** and scroll to the **bottom** for your key. Paste it in [Settings → Account and cloud](byte-setting:cloud); BYTE keeps it in your system keyring.
<!-- all -->
- Switch where answers come from: **This Mac**, **Cloud**, or **Both** side by side.
- If the cloud can't be reached, BYTE answers on your Mac instead.
- **Private chats** never go to the cloud.
