# First-run setup: models and optional features

The first time BYTE opens it walks you through a short setup. Nothing in it switches anything on by itself: every optional feature starts off, and each one only turns on if you tick it.

## Pick your models
After BYTE checks your computer you choose the chat model, the brain that answers you. BYTE's own pick is the first card.
<!-- win -->
Below the list there is one more offer: the small model that plans **PC control** (what to click to "open Notepad"). The sizes of both downloads are shown, and the button adds them up. Ticking it only downloads the model; it does not turn PC control on.
<!-- all -->

## The optional features
After the download you are asked once, in plain words, about each feature that is off until you choose it:
- **Hey BYTE:** listens through the microphone for the words "Hey BYTE". It needs a speech model, which you can download in [Settings → Voice and Hey BYTE](byte-setting:voice).
- **Browser:** lets BYTE open web pages itself, in a window you can watch, with a Stop button.
<!-- win -->
- **PC control:** lets BYTE open, close and click things on this PC when you ask. It asks before anything risky.
- **Screen questions:** when you ask about what is on your screen, BYTE looks at that window once, in memory only.
- **Guided help:** when you ask how to do something in a program, BYTE rings each button in turn, or does it for you.
<!-- all -->

Each has a one-sentence explanation. You can leave every box empty and press **Continue with all of these off**. Later, every one of them has its own switch in Settings, and the [privacy page](help:privacy) says what each one reads.

## If you change your mind
Everything chosen here can be changed in [Settings](byte-setting:general). Nothing you skip is remembered as "no": you can simply turn it on later.
