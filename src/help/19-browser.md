Browser features are **off until you turn them on**, in [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control).

1. **Watch BYTE browse:** BYTE does its research in a window you can see, with a bar that says "BYTE is browsing" and a **Stop** button. Closing the window also stops it.
2. **Live research:** BYTE opens sources one after another and answers from what it read, naming any page it could not open.
3. **Use Edge or Chrome:** BYTE drives a separate Edge or Chrome window with its own profile, not yours. If it cannot start, it falls back to its built-in window.

## What it never does
It asks before sending or buying anything, never types passwords, and blocks banking and similar sites. **Only these sites** and **Never these sites** let you add your own rules. **Most steps per task** limits how long it can go on.

## Activity log
Every page BYTE visited is listed (address only, never what was on the page). You can clear it.

Try it: type "research the best budget monitors in the browser".

If a page won't open, check the site is not on your blocked list.
