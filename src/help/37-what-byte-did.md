# The daily "what BYTE did" summary

Turn on **Daily "what BYTE did" summary** in [Settings → General](byte-setting:general). It is **off until you turn it on**.

## What it shows
A few plain lines for a day, for example:
- "PC control: 2 clicks, 1 typing, between 9:12 AM and 9:20 AM; 1 did not work."
- "Web: 4 searches, 6 pages read, between 10:02 AM and 10:30 AM."

It appears as **What BYTE did yesterday** at the end of your morning briefing, and Settings lists the last seven days under the switch.

## What it never shows
Only the **kind of thing** and **when**. It never shows what was searched, which page or file, what was typed, or the names of programs. It is built on your computer from BYTE's two activity logs (PC control and tools), and nothing is sent anywhere.

The logs themselves, with more detail, are in Settings → Privacy (the activity log) and in the PC control section, where you can also clear them. Clearing a log removes it from the summary too.
