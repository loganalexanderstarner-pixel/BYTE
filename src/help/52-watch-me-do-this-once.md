**Watch me do this once** turns something you do by hand into a routine. It is **off until you turn it on**: [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control) → **Watch me do this once**. It needs PC control to be on.

## Recording
1. Press **Start watching**. A small badge stays on screen the whole time.
2. Do the task once in your program.
3. Press **Finish recording** (here or on the badge).

BYTE notes the **buttons you press by name** and the **key combinations** you use. It never keeps what you type, never notes password or card fields, and does not watch programs on your never-touch list. Typed parts are counted and left out; add a **Type some text** step yourself if you want one.

## Saving
Give the recording a phrase and save it. It appears under **Voice routines**, where you can edit the steps, add a schedule or remove it. Say the phrase to replay it. A routine holds up to 12 steps.
