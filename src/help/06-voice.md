# Voice: talking to BYTE

- **🎤 or hold Space** in an empty message box to dictate. Needs a speech model ([Settings → Models → Voice](byte-setting:models)).
- **🔊 under any answer** reads it aloud. **Read answers aloud** does it for every answer.
- **BYTE's voice:** browse over 2,000 free voices (Kokoro, Piper, Kitten, Supertonic, Pocket) and pick one. They run on your Mac's CPU only while BYTE talks. Choose **Calm, Natural or Lively**.
- **Talk with BYTE** (the sound-wave button) is hands-free: speak, pause, hear the answer, speak again. Say "stop" to end.
- **"Hey BYTE"** (off until you turn it on) opens Quick Ask from anywhere and answers out loud. With **Open at login** on, it's ready as soon as you log in.
- Attached **recordings** become transcripts, with speaker labels ("Speaker 1, Speaker 2").
