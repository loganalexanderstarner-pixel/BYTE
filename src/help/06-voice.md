# Voice: talking to BYTE

- **🎤 or hold Space** in an empty message box to dictate. Needs a speech model ([Settings → Voice and Hey BYTE](byte-setting:voice)).
- **🔊 under any answer** reads it aloud. **Read answers aloud** does it for every answer.
- **BYTE's voice:** browse over 2,000 free voices (Kokoro, Piper, Kitten, Supertonic, Pocket) and pick one. They run on your Mac's CPU only while BYTE talks. Choose **Calm, Natural or Lively**.
- **Talk with BYTE** (the sound-wave button) is hands-free: speak, pause, hear the answer, speak again. Say "stop" to end.

- **"Hey BYTE"** (off until you turn it on) listens for the phrase and answers out loud from anywhere, with no window by default. With **Open BYTE at login** on, it's ready as soon as you sign in. Setup, follow-ups, the talk key and the chime are in [Using BYTE hands-free](help:hey-byte); talking over BYTE, focus, calls and games are in [Interrupting BYTE, calls and games](help:interrupting-calls-games).
<!-- all -->
- **Where "Hey BYTE" answers from** (Settings → Voice and Hey BYTE → **Answers from**, once you're connected to BYTE Cloud): **This PC** uses the model on this computer, **BYTE Cloud** uses the cloud, and **Automatic** uses the cloud while a game is running full screen or your graphics card is busy (so a game doesn't stutter and the answer comes quickly) and this PC otherwise. Talk mode follows the same choice. Without a BYTE Cloud account, this PC answers.
- **Things you can say after "Hey BYTE":** "turn the volume down a bit", "set a timer for ten minutes", "what's the weather in Dover, Tennessee", "open Spotify to my gaming playlist" (tell BYTE a playlist's share link once: "my gaming playlist is …"), "what's taking up space on my PC", "find my resume".
- Attached **recordings** become transcripts, with speaker labels ("Speaker 1, Speaker 2").
