# Getting started

BYTE is an AI assistant that runs on your Mac. Your chats, files and notes stay here; only web searches and downloads use the internet.

1. **Pick a model** in [Settings → Models](byte-setting:models). BYTE recommends one that fits your Mac's memory. Download it once; after that it works offline.
2. **Ask anything** in the message box. Press **Enter** to send, **Shift+Enter** for a new line.
3. **Choose a mode** under the message box: **Fast** for quick replies, **Auto** to let BYTE decide, **Deep** and **Extended** for careful, researched answers.

<!-- mac -->
> **Tip:** Press **⌘K** to find any chat, setting, mode or theme, and **⌥Space** to ask from any app (Quick Ask).
<!-- pc -->
> **Tip:** Press **Ctrl+K** to find any chat, setting, mode or theme, and **Ctrl+Alt+Space** to ask from any app (Quick Ask).
<!-- all -->

**Make it yours** in [Settings → Appearance](byte-setting:appearance): 20 themes, or **Make your own** from five colors (BYTE warns you if text would be hard to read), text size, **Reduce motion**, and an optional soft chime when an answer is ready. Your themes can be exported and shared as `.bytetheme` files.

**Updates:** BYTE checks once a day and tells you when a new version is out; install it in one click in [Settings → About](byte-setting:about). Each update is checked against BYTE's signature before it's installed.

<!-- mac -->
If macOS says BYTE "can't be opened", right-click BYTE in Applications, choose **Open**, then **Open Anyway** (see [Troubleshooting](help:troubleshooting)).
<!-- win -->
If Windows says it "protected your PC" when you first open BYTE, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway** (see [Troubleshooting](help:troubleshooting)).
