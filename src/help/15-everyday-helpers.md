# Everyday helpers, backup and problem reports

Where things are in Settings: the left list has **General**, **Models and speed**, **Voice and Hey BYTE**, **Control and screen**, **Privacy**, **Memory and notes**, **Account and cloud** and **Backup and help**. The box at the top, **Search settings**, hides everything that doesn't match the words you type.

## Saying "Hey BYTE" with no window

<!-- win -->
- "Hey BYTE" answers out loud and shows nothing on screen by default. Turn on **Show the window when you say Hey BYTE** in [the Hey BYTE settings](byte-setting:voice) if you'd rather see it.
<!-- all -->
- You can talk over BYTE to stop it, and ask a follow-up straight away without saying the wake phrase again.
- Say "good morning" and BYTE reads your daily briefing, the same one "give me my briefing" gives.
- Say "how do you say good night in Spanish" and BYTE says the translation out loud.
- **Spoken morning briefing** (off until you turn it on, with a time you choose in [the Hey BYTE settings](byte-setting:voice)): every day BYTE builds your briefing, saves it as a chat and says the summary out loud.
- Money: BYTE has no exchange-rate feed of its own. With web search on, "what's 40 euros in dollars" reads today's rate from the web; offline, BYTE can only say it has no live rates.

## Games and calls

- **Game-aware:** while a full-screen game is running, BYTE answers spoken questions from BYTE Cloud (if you're connected), plays no chime after "Hey BYTE" and holds back spoken notices (timers still ring).
- **Quiet during calls:** "Hey BYTE" stays off while another program is using the microphone for a call, and spoken notices wait. BYTE only checks which program holds the microphone; it doesn't record anything.
- **Spoken notices** (off until you turn it on): BYTE says "your model finished downloading" and when a long job is done. Only when speech is on, and never during a game or a call.

## Where did I leave off, and quick notes

- Ask "where did I leave off?" for a recap of your latest chats, notes and open to-dos. BYTE builds it from what is saved on this computer; nothing is made up.
- Say or type "note: call the dentist" (also "make a note…", "note to self…") to save a note in the Inbox folder of your Notes. "To-do: renew passport" adds a task instead.
- The switch is **Where did I leave off** in [Settings → Memory and notes](byte-setting:memory). The **memory** setting there decides what BYTE remembers about you; you can see and delete every memory.

## Several people, one BYTE

- Once BYTE has been taught your voices, **Auto** keeps a separate picture of how each person talks (short or long messages, polite or casual) and answers each one in their own style. A voice BYTE doesn't know uses the general picture. Nothing you wrote is kept, only numbers.

## Free up space

- Before a model download, and when the disk is low, BYTE says in plain words how much is needed, how much is free and which of its own files could go. **Free up space** in [Settings → Models and speed](byte-setting:models) lists them, each with a delete button that asks first. BYTE never deletes anything by itself.

## Save and restore your settings and chats

- In [Settings → Backup and help](byte-setting:backup), **Save a copy** writes your settings and chats to a file you choose, sealed with a passphrase (at least 8 characters). Keep the passphrase: without it the file can't be opened.
- **Restore from a file** asks for the passphrase, then BYTE restarts and puts the chats and settings back. Chats with the same name are replaced by the saved version.
- Your BYTE Cloud key, your account details and the kids PIN are never in the file, so after restoring on a new computer you connect to BYTE Cloud again.

## Problem report

- **Problem report** in [Settings → Backup and help](byte-setting:backup) collects BYTE's version, your system and hardware names and the end of BYTE's log. Your name, your user folder and anything that looks like a key are taken out first.
- You see exactly what will be saved, can edit it, and then save it to a file to send along when you ask for help. Nothing is sent anywhere by BYTE.

## PC control, finding files and screen questions

- **PC control and routines:** "turn the volume down a bit", "set a timer for ten minutes", "open my gaming playlist". See PC control in [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control). Every change shows an approval card first.
- **Find files:** "find my tax return pdf" searches by name and by the words inside documents.
- **Screen questions:** when you turn on the screen features, you can ask "what's on my screen?" and BYTE looks only when you ask. A replay shows what it looked at. Screen and control features are off until you turn them on, and never run in private chats.
