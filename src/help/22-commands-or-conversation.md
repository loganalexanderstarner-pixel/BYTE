BYTE can act on what you say, or just talk. **How BYTE handles commands** (in [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control)) decides:

| Setting | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Smart** (default) | Clear commands run ("set the volume to 30", "open Spotify"). Everything else is a conversation ("how do I turn off the Wi-Fi?", "what do you think of this?"). |
| **Always** | Anything that matches a command runs, even when you were only talking about it. |
| **Chat only** | BYTE never acts on its own. Start with **do:** to make it act, like "do: set the volume to 30". |

It works the same typed or spoken. Try it: with Smart on, say "how do I turn the volume down?" (an answer) and then "turn the volume down a bit" (an action).
