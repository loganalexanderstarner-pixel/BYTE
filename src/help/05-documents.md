# Documents

Ask for a **PDF report, a slide deck or a Word document** ("make a 10-slide deck about…"), or use the 📄 button.

1. BYTE writes an **outline** first. Edit, reorder or remove sections.
2. Approve it, and BYTE writes each section, with charts and tables where they help.
3. Preview, pick a theme, and **Save as** PDF, PPTX or DOCX.

With BYTE Cloud connected you can also make documents on the cloud, with more templates.
