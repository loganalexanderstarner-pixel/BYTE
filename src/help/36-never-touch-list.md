One list, in [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control) → **Never touch**, is shared by everything that looks at or operates your computer:
- **PC control** and **guided help** will not click, type, press keys in, focus, close or open anything listed.
- The **screen features** (screen questions, the replay, Read it to me, the game advisor) will not look at it.

## What is on it
The starting list holds password managers (1Password, Bitwarden, KeePass, LastPass and others), the Windows credential and security windows, private and InPrivate browser windows, and banking sites and programs. BYTE's own window is always left alone, and cannot be taken off the list.

## How it matches
Each line is part of a program's name or of a window's title, in any capitals, matched as whole words: "chase" matches "Chase Online" but not "purchase". Add a line for anything you want left alone, for example the name of a chat program. **Reset to the starting list** puts the defaults back.

## What happens
When a step would touch something on the list, BYTE stops and says so. It does not try again and does not make a new plan around it, and the activity log shows the refusal.
