# Personality

Change how BYTE talks in [Settings → About → Personality](byte-setting:about):

- **Warmth:** matter-of-fact to warm.
- **Length:** brief to thorough.
- **Humor:** none to playful.
- **Formality:** casual to formal.
- **Opinions:** neutral to opinionated.

Or pick a preset: **Balanced** (BYTE's usual voice), **Friendly**, **Concise**, **Professional**, **Teacher** or **Coach**. **Try it** sends a sample question in a private chat.
