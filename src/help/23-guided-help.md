When you ask how to do something in the program in front of you, BYTE can offer a guide. It is **off until you turn it on**: [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control) → **Guided help**. It is also off in private chats and kids mode.

## The two choices
- **Show me as I go:** BYTE draws a ring around each button in turn and waits while you do it. A click inside the ring moves on. **F8** goes to the next step, **F9** looks at the screen again, **Esc** stops. You can also say "next", "skip", "look again" or "stop".
- **Do it for me:** BYTE does the same steps itself, with PC control's rules: approval cards, run limits, the activity log and the Stop bar.

## What it sees
Only while a guide is running, BYTE reads the window in front as a list of buttons and boxes. Password and card fields are hidden from BYTE and never ringed or clicked.

## Try it
1. Turn on **Guided help**.
2. Open a program such as Settings.
3. Ask "How do I turn on dark mode?" and choose **Show me as I go**.

It is new and has not been tried on many programs yet. If the ring is in the wrong place, press **F9** to look again, or stop and ask again.
