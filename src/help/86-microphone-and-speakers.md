# Choosing your microphone and speakers

<!-- mac -->
This is part of the Windows app for now. The Mac app uses the microphone and speakers the Mac itself uses.
<!-- linux -->
This isn't available on Linux yet, so there is nothing to set up here.
<!-- win -->
By default BYTE uses the microphone and speakers Windows uses, and almost everyone should leave it that way. If you have more than one (a headset and a webcam, speakers and a monitor), you can pick in [Settings → Voice and Hey BYTE](byte-setting:voice), under **Microphone** and **Speakers**. These show once **Listen for "Hey BYTE"** is on.

## Test my microphone
Press **Test** and say "Hey BYTE". BYTE listens for five seconds with a moving bar that shows how loud you are, then says what it heard and whether that would have woken it. Nothing is recorded or kept.

- **"BYTE couldn't open a microphone":** none is plugged in, or Windows is not letting BYTE use it (Windows Settings → Privacy → Microphone).
- **"BYTE heard nothing":** the bar never moved. Check the microphone chosen above, its volume, and that it is not muted.
- **"That wouldn't wake BYTE":** it heard you but not the phrase. Say "Hey BYTE" a little clearer, or closer to the microphone.

## If the device you chose goes away
If a chosen microphone or speaker is unplugged, BYTE uses the system's default until it comes back. Changing the microphone restarts "Hey BYTE" on the new one by itself.
