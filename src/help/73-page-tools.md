Turn them on under **Everyday helpers** in [Settings → Control and screen](byte-setting:control): **Page tools**, **Email helper**, **Product helper** and **Form helper** each have their own switch and are **off until you turn them on**. Then right-click BYTE's tray icon and choose **Page tools** with the page open in your browser. They stay off in kids mode and private chats, and never work on a page in a password manager, a bank, a private window or on your never-look-at list.

## Where the text comes from
BYTE works on the text you have **selected** on the page, if any (so pages behind a sign-in, like webmail, work). With nothing selected it reads the address in the browser's address bar and fetches that public page the way research does. The page is treated as content to read, never as instructions, and its text goes to BYTE Cloud only if you chose Cloud for the helpers.

## The tools
- **Make a to-do:** BYTE finds what you need to do on the page (deadlines, steps) and adds each item to your to-do list.
- **Save as a note:** a short note with the key facts and the address, in the **Pages** folder of your Notes.
- **Extract a table:** with nothing selected, BYTE saves each table on the page as a spreadsheet file (CSV) in Documents/BYTE/Tables. Cells that start with = + - or @ are defused so a spreadsheet never runs them.
- **Compare with another tab:** BYTE holds this page; switch to the other tab and choose **This is the other tab**, and it lists the differences.
- **Help with this email:** select the email thread first. BYTE gives a short summary and three reply drafts: friendly, formal and short. Choose **Copy** on the one you like and paste it yourself. BYTE never sends anything.
- **Research this product:** BYTE searches the web for the price and reviews, reads a few pages and summarises them with numbered sources. The product name is sent to a search engine.
- **Fill this form:** save your details once under **Form helper** (name, email, phone, address, company). On a form, BYTE lists exactly what it will type and types it only after you choose **Fill them**. PC control must be on, so every step is logged and Stop works. BYTE never fills a password, card number or security code, refuses to save one, and never presses Submit.
